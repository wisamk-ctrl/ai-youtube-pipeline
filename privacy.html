Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

AI This Week ("we") is a weekly AI news show run by Wissam Kishek. We use a small internal publishing tool (the "App") that posts our own videos to our own social media accounts. This policy explains what the App does with data.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the show's team to publish to the show's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not let anyone else sign in.

What data the App handles

  • Account authorisation: when we connect our own TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn or Instagram account, the platform gives the App an access token. Tokens are stored encrypted as secrets in our build system and are used only to post our videos and to read basic information about our own account, such as its name.
  • Our own content: the videos, captions and hashtags we publish.
  • Public comments on our posts: where a platform allows it, the App reads comments on our own posts so we can reply to viewers.

The App does not collect data about viewers beyond what the platforms show publicly on our own posts. We do not sell or share data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention

Access tokens are kept until they expire or we disconnect the account. You can ask us to delete any comment data relating to you.

Contact

Message us through our LinkedIn page or our YouTube channel.
