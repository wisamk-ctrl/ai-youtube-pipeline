[Section: Anthropic Goes Public at $83]

[Visual: stock glowing sphere of connections, AI model]
Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI models, is now a public company.

[Visual: stock coins falling, dark background, animation]
Yahoo Finance reports it sold 980 million shares at $83 each, above the $70 to $78 range it had marketed.

[Visual: number $1.5 trillion | valuation at the IPO price]
That valued Anthropic at about $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis.

[Visual: number $97.17 | first-day closing share price]
The BBC says shares closed 17% higher on day one at $97.17, even as the wider tech sector fell 2.4%.

[Visual: stock data center server racks, blue light, animation]
Third-quarter revenue hit $16.96 billion, up 47% from the prior quarter. But the company still runs at an operating loss.

[Visual: stock data center construction blueprint, digital animation]
It has committed to spending about $895 billion, with roughly half promised by 2030.

[Visual: stock three data centers linked by bridges of light]
For the Gulf, watch whether this new capital flows into data centers and compute partnerships across the region.
