[Section: The U.S. Tech Outage Wave]

[Visual: stock office tower at night, floors going dark]
Foreign Policy reports a wave of tech outages across the U.S. government and private sector.

[Visual: stock server hall with one dark row of racks]
A global Microsoft outage that began last Thursday disrupted services that businesses, schools and governments rely on.

[Visual: stock network traffic jamming at a gateway ring]
On Friday, the FAA issued a rare nationwide ground stop, causing widespread flight cancellations and delays.

[Visual: stock glass payment cards linked by gold light]
Chase Bank, Bank of America and Apple services also faced brief disruptions, and some internal NSA systems went down.

[Visual: title No evidence of cyberattacks]
Foreign Policy says there is currently no evidence of cyberattacks, or that the incidents were linked.

[Visual: stock data center server racks, blue light, animation]
Microsoft's Charles Lamanna cited a latent bug, a surge in load and reduced server capacity.

[Visual: stock data center corridor, blue light]
For the Gulf, the question to watch is how much critical services depend on a few shared platforms.
