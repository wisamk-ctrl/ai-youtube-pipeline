# Lessons from past episodes (read with the style guide; every writer, reviewer and critic pass sees this)

Each lesson comes from a real fault heard or caught in a past episode. Treat a repeat as a MAJOR defect.

1. **A name is said once.** W45 voiced «أوبن إيه آي OpenAI» as two names, eleven times. Write the Arabic
   spoken form with the Latin in braces: «أوبن إيه آي{OpenAI}». Never Arabic + bare Latin.
2. **No English in brackets.** «(AI agents)», «(on-behalf-of)», «(sovereign AI)» were all read aloud in W45.
   If a term needs explaining, explain it in Ammani words or put it on screen.
3. **Letters-and-digits names need a spoken form.** G42 was read «جي اتنان وأربعون»; write «جي فورتي تو».
   Claude was read "cloud": write «كلود». HUMAIN was spelled letter by letter: write «هيوماين».
4. **No vowel marks, and no ambiguous words.** «عن بُعد» was read «عم بوعد», and without marks «بعد» reads
   as "after". Say «من بعيد».
5. **Never say anything about this channel's own tools or lists unless it is checked.** W45 said Tencent was
   not on our watchlist; it was (as "Tencent Hunyuan"). The radar is a lead, not a source: cite the reports
   behind it, never «الرادار» as evidence, and make no claim about what we do or do not track.
6. **One product name per idea.** «صلاحيات IAM… PrivateLink… CloudTrail» in one sentence is a list of
   foreign sounds to the ear. Name the one that matters; describe the rest.
7. **Version numbers are not spoken.** «2.1.317» goes on screen; say «آخر إصدار».
8. **One figure per breath.** «عشرة فاصلة تمانية ثانية مقابل تمانية وتمانين فاصلة أربعة» lost listeners;
   give the comparison in words («أسرع بحوالي تمن مرات») and put both numbers on screen.
9. **Vendor figures are the vendor's.** Six NVIDIA numbers in a row read as an advert. Say whose number it is
   in the same sentence, and keep to four vendor figures a segment.
10. **One story in the cold open.** W45 opened with three headlines in 20 seconds. One story, one number,
    one question to the viewer, then the promise of what's coming.
11. **Vary «ليش بيهمّنا».** W45 used it seven times. At most three, in different words.
12. **The region is the edge.** Regional stories (TII, BAREC, SDAIA) got one line each while vendor blogs got
    minutes. Lead with what changes something for people here.
13. **Formal Arabic leaks in.** «بالمقابل», «مقارنة بـ», «الماء», «بشكل متوافق مع» were flagged in W45.
    Use the Ammani table in the style guide.
