[Section: Brad Smith Apologises for Outage]

[Visual: number 24 hours | Microsoft global outage]
Microsoft president Brad Smith has apologised again for a global outage that CBS said lasted 24 hours.

[Visual: stock server hall with one dark row of racks]
Smith told CBS it was not linked to AI, and it was not a cyberattack.

[Visual: stock network traffic jamming at a gateway ring]
He blamed a perfect storm: software updated four months earlier, a new update and a shift in network traffic.

[Visual: stock data center corridor, blue light]
Microsoft has published two reports in the last 10 days, showing minute by minute what happened.

[Visual: number 13 | more steps Microsoft plans to take]
Smith says the company has taken nine steps already, with 13 more to come.

[Visual: stock layered technology stack animation, chips and data centers]
He called the wider wave of outages partly AI ripple effects, as AI made flaws in code easier to find.
