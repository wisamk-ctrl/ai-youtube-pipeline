[Section: Microsoft Shuts Down To Do App]

[Visual: number 10 years | To Do's run before shutdown]
Microsoft is shutting down To Do, its task-manager app, after nearly 10 years.

[Visual: title To Do ends June 30, 2027]
The company says the shutdown takes place on June 30, 2027, across Windows, Mac, Android, iOS and the web.

[Visual: stock server hall with one dark row of racks]
Existing tasks and lists will not migrate to Microsoft Planner, the new home for work and school tasks.

[Visual: stock empty boardroom table, dark blue light]
Planner is not available on personal accounts, including Microsoft 365 Personal and Family subscribers. They will need another app.

[Visual: stock data center corridor, blue light]
Microsoft says it will offer an export option, but has not yet published the details.

[Visual: stock network traffic jamming at a gateway ring]
The underlying To Do service and APIs will be retired separately, at a date not yet disclosed.

[Visual: stock office tower at night, floors going dark]
For Gulf companies running on Microsoft tools, watch how smoothly teams move their task data before the deadline.
