[Section: Jakub Pachocki Urges AI Limits]

[Visual: stock glowing sphere of connections, AI model]
OpenAI's chief scientist Jakub Pachocki wants rival AI labs to agree on limits for their most powerful models.

[Visual: stock open model library, glowing data cubes]
In an essay published November 22, he proposes common limits based on perplexity and other measurements.

[Visual: stock chips linked by beams of gold light]
Perplexity scores how well a model guesses the next piece of text.

[Visual: number under 0.8 | Perplexity of OpenAI's newest models]
OpenAI published its own scores for the first time, falling from about 0.99 for GPT-3.5 to under 0.8 for its newest models.

[Visual: stock GPU server racks close-up, violet and gold]
Labs would stay below the limits until they can understand and monitor model behavior. CEO Sam Altman backed the idea.

[Visual: title No Value, No Date Yet]
Reports name no limit value, no start date, and no body to run it.

[Visual: stock data center corridor, blue light]
For Gulf governments building AI capacity, watch whether shared limits become a standard they're expected to follow.
