[Section: Microsoft 365 Goes Down Globally]

[Visual: stock office tower at night, floors going dark]
BankInfoSecurity reports a global Microsoft 365 outage disrupted email, calendars and cloud tools across many sectors this week.

[Visual: number 7 million | Downdetector reports of the outage]
InformationWeek reports it lasted over 20 hours, with more than 7 million reports on Downdetector.

[Visual: stock server hall with one dark row of racks]
That makes it the largest outage reported there in the last 12 months.

[Visual: stock data center corridor, blue light]
Microsoft says the disruption was not caused by malicious actors.

[Visual: stock network traffic jamming at a gateway ring]
But Microsoft Threat Intelligence says it saw a coordinated, high-volume phishing campaign targeting enterprises globally.

[Visual: stock empty boardroom table, dark blue light]
Experts say U.S. hospitals largely kept patient care running, using manual workarounds. For many, services returned by early Friday.

[Visual: title Gulf cloud resilience to watch]
For Gulf businesses and governments relying on a single cloud provider, the question to watch is whether continuity plans can cover outages like this.
