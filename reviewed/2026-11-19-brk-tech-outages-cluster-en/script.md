[Section: A Week of Major Tech Outages]

[Visual: stock office tower at night, floors going dark]
In one week, Microsoft, Apple, JPMorgan Chase, United Airlines and the NSA all suffered major tech failures, Scientific American reports.

[Visual: number 24 hours | Microsoft cloud software unavailable]
Microsoft's cloud software was unavailable for almost 24 hours.

[Visual: stock mobile network towers over a city at night, gold signal waves]
A malfunctioning telecom network halted U.S. air traffic.

[Visual: stock glowing sphere of connections, AI model]
Some blamed cyberattacks or AI running amok. The magazine says the evidence supports neither.

[Visual: stock server hall with one dark row of racks]
Microsoft says AI agents weren't the cause. Apple's outage followed an internal update, and the NSA cites scheduled maintenance.

[Visual: stock data center corridor, blue light]
The Uptime Institute found data center outage rates per site fell in 2026 for a fifth year running.

[Visual: stock network traffic jamming at a gateway ring]
Reliability engineer Laura Nolan says outages can cluster in a few days without a shared cause.

[Visual: stock data center server racks, blue light, animation]
For Gulf businesses and governments, the thing to watch is how cloud providers test and manage updates to ever more complex systems.
