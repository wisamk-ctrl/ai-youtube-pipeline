## Tier 2 — first month
- **Encode chain**: overlays + subtitles in one final high-quality pass (crf 16–18, `+faststart`), lossless intermediates; stock is re-encoded 4×, `burn_in` uses defaults. (M)
- **Animated cards**, still shots ≤ 5 s, dissolves at section boundaries. (M)
- **Subtitles**: translucent box, ~54 px, brand font, Unicode isolates around Latin runs; lift lower-thirds clear of captions. (S)
- **Thumbnail + end screen**: render from `thumbnail_text_ar`; 15 s end card. (M)
- **Description builder**: Arabic hook and headlines, `00:00` chapters from sections, English `localizations`; upload the script SRT as a caption track. (S)
- **Shorts**: native vertical layouts (centre crop cuts the cards), 2–3 per episode, linked to the episode; vary formats (YouTube's 2025 "inauthentic / mass-produced" rule). (M)
- **Story mix**: max one segment per vendor blog; add Levant sources (MoDEE, QCRI/Fanar, WAM, SPA, The National, Arab News, Wamda); hype YouTube channels only as radar leads. Needs the open audience decision. (M)
- **Rubric additions**: vendor figures need a spoken caveat; ≤ 4 vendor figures per segment; «ليش بيهمّنا» varied (7× in W45); no pause between a preposition and its number; ≥ 3 unglossed Latin names in a sentence is MAJOR; MSA→Ammani table («بالمقابل», «الماء», «مدوّنة»). (S)
- **Repair quality**: cut at the quietest point between words, 20–30 ms crossfades, room tone instead of digital silence, LUFS matching on speech only. (M)
- **CI**: cache the Whisper model and Chromium; save `heard.json` so a post re-run skips Whisper; lock dependencies; failure issues carry the error text. (S each)
- **Paste pack**: split scenes only at sections (never between a question and its answer); the old W45 pack still names the replaced look. (S)
