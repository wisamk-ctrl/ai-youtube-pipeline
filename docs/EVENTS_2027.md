## Media accreditation: how to apply

General points:

- **Apply as "Online Media / Creator" wherever that category exists.** GITEX Africa has it
  explicitly ("Online Media / Blogger"). Cairo ICT accepts "accredited content creators". Step
  accepts "podcasters and creators". Web Summit and LEAP limit passes to editorial or verified
  media, so lead with the show's editorial role.
- **Prepare a standard media kit once and reuse it:** channel URLs (YouTube and LinkedIn),
  subscriber and view figures, 2–3 sample episodes, the presenter's name and role, an
  official-domain email address (LEAP requires a company or work email), and passport or national
  ID scans (GITEX Africa and GAIN registration ask for ID).
- **No event published a hard media deadline** on the pages fetched. Web Summit Qatar reviews
  weekly, so apply early. Most GITEX / DWTC-network events close registration shortly before the
  show, so apply as soon as the 2027 form opens.
- **Media partnership is a separate route** from a press pass. It is a co-promotion deal (logo
  placement and social posts in exchange for access and visibility), offered by Step, Ai Everything,
  the GITEX family, Expand North Star, Web Summit and Dubai AI Festival. It suits a show that can
  promise pre-event coverage.
