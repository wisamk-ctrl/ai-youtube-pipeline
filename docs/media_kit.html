AI This Week

أسبوع الذكاء الاصطناعي

The week's AI news, explained for the Middle East.

A weekly YouTube show that takes the biggest AI stories of the week and asks what they mean for companies, governments and people in the region. A full episode every Sunday, and a Short the same day when a big story breaks.

Every story, three beats

What happened

The facts, each one traced to a named source.

Why it matters

Analysis and context. At least half of every episode is explanation, not a reading of the news.

The regional lens

What it means here: a customer, a regulator, a build-out, a cost.

Editorial standards

  • Every claim is traced to a named source before an episode is published.
  • Informational only. No investment advice, stock calls or price targets.
  • No third-party video clips and no invented quotes or figures.
  • The presenter on screen is a digital twin of the host, and every video description says so.
  • Sponsors never shape the reporting. Paid placements are labelled as paid promotion on YouTube.
