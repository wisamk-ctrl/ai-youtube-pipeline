[Visual: stock office tower at night, floors going dark]
Microsoft faces a pair of class actions in Canada over last week's massive outage, Courthouse News reports.

[Visual: stock server hall with one dark row of racks]
One suit, filed in Quebec Superior Court, says consumers were blocked from paid Microsoft services on November 12 and 13.

[Visual: stock data center corridor, blue light]
It claims Microsoft should have anticipated the outage, given other major outages in 2025.

[Visual: stock data center server racks, blue light, animation]
Microsoft blames a latent defect from July and reduced server capacity. It says it was not a cyberattack.

[Visual: title Second suit includes businesses]
A second suit, by Metus Lykos Debt Law, seeks to represent all Canadians, including businesses.

[Visual: number 24-hour | Email blackout, says the lawyer]
Attorney Darryl Singer says a 24-hour email blackout means lost customers and missed deadlines.
