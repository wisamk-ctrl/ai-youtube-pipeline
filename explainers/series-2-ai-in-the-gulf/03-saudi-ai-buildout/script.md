In late 2025, Saudi Arabia's national AI company said its NVIDIA project would include about [Pause] 600,000 chips. Washington had approved exports of only a small fraction of that. By the end, you'll know how to tell AI that's planned from AI that's plugged in.

[Section: From strategy to champion]

Let's start with how the kingdom got here, from a strategy document to a national company.

[On-Screen Text: SDAIA, Saudi Data & AI Authority]

The first building block is SDAIA, the Saudi Data and AI Authority. It was set up by Royal Order and reports directly to the Prime Minister.

I'm Wissam, and this is AI This Week, AI Explained.

[Pause]

SDAIA has three arms. One is the National Center for AI, known as NCAI. Remember that name; it matters later.

In July 2020, the National Strategy for Data and AI was approved. It was unveiled that October, at the first Global AI Summit in Riyadh.

[Pause]

[On-Screen Text: 2030 targets: top 15 in AI | 20,000+ specialists | ~SAR 75 billion investment | 300+ startups]

According to SDAIA, its 2030 targets include a place among the top 15 AI countries. [Pause] They also include more than 20,000 data and AI specialists. SDAIA cites about [Pause] 75 billion riyals of investment, and over 300 startups.

Then came Arabic AI. NCAI developed ALLaM, a family of large language models. A large language model is software trained on huge amounts of text, so it can read and write. The ALLaM research paper went public in July 2024.

[Pause]

[On-Screen Text: HUMAIN | launched May 12, 2025]

Then, on May 12, 2025, Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman announced HUMAIN. HUMAIN was launched under PIF, the Public Investment Fund. PIF says HUMAIN's job is to build the entire AI stack. That means data centres, cloud infrastructure, models and applications. The Crown Prince chairs HUMAIN. Its CEO is Tareq Amin.

[Pause]

His ambition, as he told CNBC: "We want to be the third-largest AI provider in the world, behind the United States and China."

[Section: The deal sheet]

Next, the partners, and the gap between chips announced and chips approved.

[Pause]

First, two terms. A GPU is a graphics chip. It does the heavy maths that trains and runs AI. Data centre size is measured in power. A megawatt powers a big building. A gigawatt is a thousand megawatts.

[On-Screen Text: NVIDIA: up to 500 MW | first phase 18,000 GB300 chips (May 2025)]

In May 2025, NVIDIA and HUMAIN announced AI factories of up to [Pause] 500 megawatts. They would run on several hundred thousand NVIDIA GPUs over five years. The first phase is a supercomputer with 18,000 GB300 chips, NVIDIA's high-end AI processors.

By November 2025, HUMAIN said the project would include about [Pause] 600,000 GPUs. Elon Musk's xAI would be its first customer.

[On-Screen Text: AMD $10B / 500 MW | AWS "$5B-plus" AI Zone | Qualcomm 200 MW | xAI 500 MW]

AMD and HUMAIN said they would invest up to $10 billion. The aim is 500 megawatts over five years, at sites from Saudi Arabia to the United States.

[Pause]

AWS announced a planned investment of "$5 billion-plus" in an AI Zone in Saudi Arabia. That is separate from Amazon's own Saudi cloud region, which Amazon puts at $5.3 billion. AWS also committed with PIF to train 100,000 Saudis in cloud and generative AI.

[Pause]

Qualcomm's deal is about inference. Inference means running a trained model to answer questions, rather than training it. HUMAIN is targeting 200 megawatts of Qualcomm inference racks from 2026.

[Pause]

xAI plans a 500 megawatt data centre with HUMAIN. In February 2026, HUMAIN also announced a $3 billion investment in xAI. That stake was later converted into SpaceX shares.

[Pause]

There's more. Groq, an inference-chip maker, got a $1.5 billion Saudi commitment in February 2025, before HUMAIN existed. CNBC lists Groq among HUMAIN's partners.

[Pause]

In August 2026, Microsoft said it plans to offer ALLaM through Microsoft Foundry, its platform for businesses to build with AI models. ALLaM would also come to the Microsoft 365 Copilot ecosystem.

[Pause]

Now the catch. Advanced US chips need export approval. Export controls are government rules on who may receive sensitive technology. In November 2025, Fortune reports, Washington authorised up to [Pause] 35,000 GB300 chips for HUMAIN. Announced plans are far larger than approved shipments.

[Section: Power, land and money]

This chapter covers how big it's meant to get, where it's being built, and who pays.

[On-Screen Text: Saudi data centre capacity: 68 MW (2021) → 467 MW (Q1 2026)]

Start with the base. Official Saudi data cited by Fortune puts national data centre capacity in 2021 at [Pause] 68 megawatts. By early 2026, it had reached 467 megawatts.

[On-Screen Text: HUMAIN targets: 1.9 GW (earlier) | up to 3 GW by 2030 (owners, Nov 2026) | ~6 GW by 2034]

HUMAIN's 2030 target has been reported as 1.9 gigawatts. Its owners now cite [Pause] 3 gigawatts by 2030. The sources don't explain the difference. They may be counting different things. Further out, several outlets report about 6 gigawatts by 2034. S&P gives a range up to 6.6.

[Pause]

On the ground, HUMAIN broke ground in Riyadh and Dammam in August 2025. Each site was expected to open in the second quarter of 2026 with 100 megawatts. None of the sources we checked confirms they went live.

[Pause]

[On-Screen Text: NEOM / Oxagon: HUMAIN ~100 MW, due 2028]

On the Red Sea coast, DataVolt plans a 1.5 gigawatt AI campus at Oxagon, in NEOM. HUMAIN agreed to develop about 100 megawatts of the campus's first phase, due in 2028. [Pause] Nebius signed a preliminary framework for up to 50 megawatts. Reuters notes that NEOM plans have been scaled back amid cost overruns.

[Pause]

Here's the easiest way to read all these numbers. A megawatt target is like a restaurant booking for 600 guests. It tells you the plan. What counts is how many chairs are in the room tonight: power connected, chips delivered and approved. [Pause] Then, how many diners actually turn up: customers like xAI or Nebius.

[On-Screen Text: Ownership since Nov 1, 2026: PIF 51% | Aramco 49%]

Now the money. HUMAIN doesn't disclose an overall investment target. In November 2026, Aramco completed its purchase of 49 percent. PIF keeps 51 percent, so HUMAIN is now majority PIF-owned. Aramco says it contributes about [Pause] 9.9 billion riyals in cash in 2026, plus AI and compute assets.

[Pause]

Debt is arriving too. HUMAIN signed 2.5 billion riyals of financing with the National Infrastructure Fund. Reuters reported HUMAIN hired Goldman Sachs to arrange at least 20 billion riyals more. [Pause] And Fortune says Amin is preparing for a stock market listing, an IPO. He has spoken of listing in Riyadh and New York by 2029. No timetable or size has been announced.

[On-Screen Text: Wissam's view]

This next part is my view, not a reported fact. [DRAFT VIEW: founder to confirm]

After 25 years selling enterprise software across this region, I've learned one thing. A signed partnership is a beginning, not a delivery. [Pause] HUMAIN's deal sheet is genuinely impressive. Few companies line up NVIDIA, AMD, AWS, Qualcomm and Microsoft within eighteen months. But the scorecard that matters is simpler. How many megawatts are energised? How many approved chips are racked and running? Which paying customers are using them? [Pause] Bringing in Aramco and outside lenders tells me the build-out is moving from vision to balance sheet. That's healthy. [Pause] So when you read the next big gigawatt headline, ask the restaurant question. How many chairs are in the room, and who's sitting in them?

[Section: Arabic AI and the people problem]

Chips are only half the story. The other half is models and people.

A model's knowledge comes from tokens: small chunks of text, roughly word pieces. The ALLaM 7B preview has seven billion parameters. Parameters are the internal settings a model adjusts while learning. [Pause] It was trained from scratch on [Pause] 5.2 trillion tokens. The training ran first on English, then on mixed Arabic and English.

[Pause]

[On-Screen Text: HUMAIN Chat, powered by ALLaM 34B (Aug 2025)]

In August 2025, HUMAIN launched HUMAIN Chat on web, iOS and Android. It runs on ALLaM 34B and is hosted end to end inside the kingdom. HUMAIN says more than 600 domain experts helped refine it.

[Pause]

Aramco has its own industrial model, METABRAIN. In February 2025, Aramco said a 70-billion-parameter version was in service. It said a one-trillion-parameter model was in development.

[On-Screen Text: SAMAI: 1 million+ trained | 50% AI hiring gap]

Then people. SDAIA's "One Million Saudis in AI" programme passed [Pause] one million citizens trained, the Saudi Press Agency reported. Fifty-two percent were women. Yet Saudi Arabia's HR minister has said AI roles face a 50 percent hiring gap. [Pause] Training at scale and hiring experts are not the same problem.

[Section: Saudi and the UAE: two playbooks]

Finally, the comparison everyone asks about, using only what the sources compare.

[On-Screen Text: Data centre watts per person: Saudi ~12 | UAE & US ~50 (Alvarez & Marsal)]

Alvarez & Marsal estimates about [Pause] 12 watts of data centre capacity per person in Saudi Arabia. It puts the UAE and the US at around 50. So the kingdom is catching up from a lower base.

[Pause]

S&P expects the two countries together to add about 2 to 3 gigawatts by 2030. It says Saudi capacity will mostly arrive later in the decade, while the UAE is accelerating. 451 Research puts average build costs at about [Pause] $11 million per megawatt in the UAE. In Saudi Arabia, it's about $13 million.

[Pause]

The UAE's 5 gigawatt AI campus is anchored by Stargate UAE, a one gigawatt cluster. [Pause] And on export rules, the UAE was moved to a US export-control category called A:5 in July 2026. Saudi Arabia has not been. [Pause] As a condition of US chip approvals, HUMAIN had to phase out Huawei equipment. So did G42, an AI group in the UAE.

[Section: The bottom line]

Three things to remember.

One: in five years, Saudi Arabia went from a national AI strategy to HUMAIN, a full-stack company. It's now majority PIF-owned, with Aramco holding 49 percent.

[Pause]

Two: the targets are huge, up to 3 gigawatts by 2030 according to its owners. But announced chips run far ahead of approved ones. Judge it by chairs in the room, not bookings.

[Pause]

Three: models like ALLaM and a million trained citizens matter. But the hiring gap and US export status will shape the pace.
