## Facts

### HUMAIN: launch, ownership, leadership, mandate
1. HUMAIN was launched in May 2025 under the Public Investment Fund (PIF) to build the entire AI stack: data centers, cloud infrastructure, models and applications [01].
2. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman announced the launch on May 12, 2025 [22]; CNBC notes it came a day before President Trump's visit to the kingdom [08].
3. HUMAIN is chaired by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman [01]; its CEO is Tareq Amin [02][08].
4. HUMAIN describes itself as working in four areas: next-generation data centers, infrastructure and cloud platforms, AI models (including ALLaM), and AI solutions for different sectors [02][20].
5. Tareq Amin told CNBC: "We want to be the third-largest AI provider in the world, behind the United States and China" [08].
6. In October 2025 PIF said the Saudi Company for Artificial Intelligence (SCAI), wholly owned by PIF, had been transferred to HUMAIN [19].
7. On 28 October 2025 PIF and Aramco signed a non-binding term sheet for Aramco to take a significant minority stake and to combine their AI programs in HUMAIN [19].
8. The deal closed on 1 November 2026: Aramco, through a wholly owned subsidiary, holds 49% and PIF keeps 51% [20][21].
9. Aramco is contributing about SAR 9.9 billion ($2.6 billion) in cash in 2026, plus AI, compute and digital assets [20].
10. Fortune reports that Amin is assembling a team to prepare an IPO. He said in October 2025 that he aimed to list in Saudi Arabia and New York by 2029. No timetable or offering size has been announced [24][22].

### HUMAIN: partnerships (as announced)
11. NVIDIA (May 13, 2025): AI factories of up to 500 MW, powered by "several hundred thousand" NVIDIA GPUs over five years. The first phase is an 18,000-chip GB300 Grace Blackwell supercomputer [02].
12. In November 2025 Humain said the NVIDIA project would include about 600,000 NVIDIA GPUs, and xAI would be its first customer [06].
13. On Nov. 19, 2025 the U.S. Commerce Department authorized exports of up to 35,000 NVIDIA Blackwell GB300 chips each to G42 and Humain, Fortune reports [31][32].
14. In October 2026 Humain announced plans with NVIDIA to deploy around 27,000 GPUs and up to 84 MW, starting in Q4 2026, with large-scale deployments from mid-2027 [31].
15. AMD (May 13, 2025): the two companies will invest up to $10B to deploy 500 MW of AI compute over five years, at sites from Saudi Arabia to the US [03].
16. In November 2025 CNBC reported that AMD would supply Instinct MI450 GPUs that may need as much as 1 gigawatt of power by 2030, with Cisco providing infrastructure [06].
17. AWS (May 13, 2025): a planned investment of "$5 billion-plus" in an AI Zone in Saudi Arabia. This is on top of Amazon's separate US$5.3 billion AWS region, due in 2026 [04].
18. AWS committed with PIF to train 100,000 Saudi citizens in cloud and generative AI [04].
19. In November 2025 Amazon and Humain announced plans for up to 150,000 AI accelerators in Riyadh [22].
20. Qualcomm (Oct 27, 2025): Humain is targeting 200 MW of Qualcomm AI200/AI250 inference racks from 2026, in Saudi Arabia and globally [05].
21. xAI (Nov 19, 2025): xAI plans a 500 MW data center with Humain [06][31]. On Feb. 18, 2026 Humain announced a $3 billion investment in xAI, which was later converted into SpaceX shares [22].
22. Groq: in February 2025 Groq secured a $1.5 billion commitment from Saudi Arabia to expand its AI inference data center in Dammam [07]. CNBC lists Groq among Humain's partners [08].
23. Microsoft (Aug 26, 2026): ALLaM is to be offered through Microsoft Foundry and the M365 Copilot ecosystem, alongside Microsoft forward-deployed engineers [26].
24. Other partners PIF lists: Google Cloud [01]. Other deals reported: a JV with stc's Center3 for up to 1 GW (starting at 250 MW), Blackstone/AirTrunk, and a 250 MW project with Together AI [22][32].

### HUMAIN: capacity and money
25. In August 2025 Humain broke ground in Riyadh and Dammam. Those sites were expected to go live in Q2 2026 with an initial 100 MW each [09].
26. In August 2025 CNBC reported two campuses of 11 data centers, each data center 200 MW. The plan was 50 MW by Q4 2025, then 50 MW more each quarter into 2026 [08].
27. The 2030 capacity target has been reported as 1.9 GW [08][24][30]. The newest official figure is 3 GW of AI data center capacity by 2030 (Aramco/PIF, Nov 2026) [20][21].
28. The longer-range target is about 6 GW by 2034 [08][24][25], or 6.0 to 6.6 GW per S&P [30]. DCD describes it as "6.6GW over the next decade" [09].
29. Humain does not disclose overall investment targets. It has announced $23 billion for strategic technology partnerships and a $10 billion venture fund [08].
30. In November 2026 Humain and the National Infrastructure Fund signed SAR 2.5 billion ($667m) of financing: a GPU facility of up to SAR 1.1 billion (about 25 MW of IT load) and SAR 1.4 billion for grid connections [23].
31. Reuters reported in May 2026 that Humain hired Goldman Sachs to arrange at least SR20 billion ($5.33 billion) of data center financing [22].
32. DCD reports Humain has more than 200 plots of land for potential data centers [23].

### ALLaM, SDAIA and the national strategy
33. SDAIA was set up by Royal Order (A/471) dated 29/12/1440 AH and reports directly to the Prime Minister. Its arms are the National Center for AI (NCAI), the National Data Management Office and the National Information Center [14].
34. The National Strategy for Data & AI (NSDAI) was approved on July 17, 2020 [15] and unveiled at the first Global AI Summit in October 2020 [16].
35. NSDAI targets for 2030: top 15 countries in AI, 20,000+ data and AI specialists, about SAR 75 billion of investment, 300+ startups, top 10 in open data, and top 20 in scientific contribution [15]. The National reported the investment target as $20 billion [16].
36. The NSDAI's priority sectors are education, government, healthcare, energy and mobility [15].
37. ALLaM was developed at SDAIA's NCAI [12]. The ALLaM paper was posted to arXiv on 22 July 2024 [13].
38. ALLaM-7B-Instruct-preview was trained from scratch on 5.2 trillion tokens: 4T English, then 1.2T mixed Arabic/English [12].
39. HUMAIN Chat launched Aug. 25, 2025 on web, iOS and Android, powered by ALLaM 34B. It is hosted end-to-end on HUMAIN infrastructure in the Kingdom [10].
40. HUMAIN says ALLaM 34B was refined with input from more than 600 domain experts and 250 evaluators. It was built by more than 120 AI specialists, including 35 PhD holders [10][11].

### PIF, Aramco Digital, NEOM
41. PIF's portfolio page cites an MCIT hyperscale data center plan of more than 1.3 GW before 2030. It puts the Saudi data center market at USD 1.9Bn in 2024, rising to USD 3.9Bn by 2030 [01].
42. Aramco Digital is Aramco's digital and technology subsidiary [27]. In September 2024 it partnered with Groq on what they called the world's largest inferencing data center in Saudi Arabia [17].
43. Aramco announced METABRAIN, its industrial LLM, at LEAP 2024 [28]. In February 2025 Aramco said a 70-billion-parameter version was in service and a one-trillion-parameter model was in development [27].
44. NEOM and DataVolt signed on 10 February 2025 for a 1.5 GW AI campus at Oxagon. DataVolt's initial investment is USD 5 billion, with phase one expected to be operational by 2028 [29].
45. In August 2026 Humain and DataVolt agreed on about 100 MW of the Oxagon first phase (360 MW), to be available in 2028 [25]. In October 2026 Nebius signed a preliminary framework for up to 50 MW there [21].
46. Reuters notes that NEOM plans have been scaled back amid cost overruns [25].

### Talent and the national picture
47. SDAIA's "One Million Saudis in AI" (SAMAI) programme passed one million citizens trained and certified (SPA, Nov 9, 2025). Participants were 52% female and 48% male [18].
48. Saudi Arabia's HR minister has said AI roles in the kingdom face a 50% hiring gap [08].
49. Official Saudi data cited by Fortune: national data center capacity grew from 68 MW in 2021 to 467 MW in Q1 2026 [24].

### Saudi vs UAE (only where a source compares them)
50. Alvarez & Marsal estimates about 12 watts of data center capacity per person in Saudi Arabia, against around 50 in the UAE and the US [24].
51. S&P expects the UAE and Saudi Arabia together to add about 2 to 3 GW of IT power by 2030. It says Saudi capacity will mostly arrive later in the decade, while the UAE is accelerating [30].
52. 451 Research puts average build costs over 2026-2030 at about $11 million per MW in the UAE and $13 million per MW in Saudi Arabia [30].
53. The UAE's 5 GW AI campus is anchored by Stargate UAE, a 1 GW cluster [30][31][32].
54. The UAE was moved to the US export Country Group A:5 in July 2026. Saudi Arabia has not been [32][24].
55. As a condition of US chip approvals, both G42 and Humain were required to phase out Huawei equipment [32].

## Conflicts and dates

- **2030 capacity target.** CNBC (Aug 2025), Fortune (Sep 2026) and Khaleej Times/S&P (Oct 2026) report 1.9 GW. Aramco/PIF (Nov 1, 2026) say "3 GW by 2030", and this is the newest figure. The two may count different things (Humain-only vs. including assets Aramco transferred; IT load vs. total power), and no fetched page explains the difference. On air: "up to 3 gigawatts by 2030, according to its owners", with 1.9 GW as the earlier figure.
- **Long-range target.** CNBC, Fortune and Reuters say about 6 GW by 2034. DCD (Aug 2025) says "6.6GW over the next decade". S&P gives a 6.0 to 6.6 GW range for 2034.
- **Ownership.** Humain was 100% PIF from launch (Arab News Aug 2025 calls it "wholly owned"). It has been 51% PIF and 49% Aramco since Nov 1, 2026. Any script line saying "PIF-owned" must now say "majority PIF-owned".
- **NVIDIA GPU count.** "Several hundred thousand" over five years (May 2025) became "about 600,000" (Nov 2025, per CNBC). US-approved exports so far are up to 35,000 GB300s (Nov 2025), and the first phase is 18,000 GB300s. Announced plans are much larger than approved shipments.
- **Arabic speakers.** SPA says "more than 400 million"; Arab News says "350 million". Avoid the number, or attribute it to the source.
- **METABRAIN size.** It was announced as 250B parameters (Mar 2024). In Feb 2025 Aramco said a 70B model was in service and a 1T model in development.
- **NSDAI investment target.** SDAIA's own page says about SAR 75 billion; The National reported $20 billion. These are roughly the same at the riyal's peg (about SAR 3.75 to the dollar), but quote SDAIA's figure in riyals.
- **PIF size.** CNBC (Aug 2025) says "nearly $1 trillion" in assets; Fortune (Sep 2026) says "$900 billion". Avoid or attribute.
- **AWS AI Zone.** The "$5 billion-plus" figure is separate from Amazon's $5.3 billion AWS region. Do not add them together or confuse them.
- **SPA Aramco Digital/Groq release (2024).** The "53 tokens per second by 2025" line looks garbled. Use only the 5.28 million tokens per second end-2024 figure, if at all.
- **Fast-changing.** Data center go-live dates (Riyadh and Dammam were due Q2 2026, and no fetched page confirms they went live), IPO timing, US export-control status, xAI/SpaceX holdings, and NVIDIA leadership. Fortune (Nov 18, 2026) reports Jensen Huang's departure from NVIDIA amid scandal. Do not describe him as NVIDIA's current CEO without re-checking, and keep the allegations off air.

## Shorts (AI in 30 seconds)

1. **"600,000 GPUs on paper."** Fact: Humain says its NVIDIA project will include about 600,000 GPUs, but the US approved exports of up to 35,000 GB300s in November 2025 [06][31]. Takeaway: in Gulf AI, watch export licences, not press-release chip counts.
2. **"Oil company buys into AI."** Fact: Aramco closed a deal on Nov 1, 2026 for 49% of HUMAIN, putting in about $2.6 billion in cash plus its AI assets; PIF keeps 51% [20]. Takeaway: Saudi AI now has the kingdom's two biggest balance sheets behind it.
3. **"12 watts vs 50."** Fact: Alvarez & Marsal estimates Saudi Arabia has about 12 watts of data-centre capacity per person, against around 50 in the UAE and US [24]. Takeaway: the Saudi build-out is a catch-up race, so delivery speed is the metric to watch.
