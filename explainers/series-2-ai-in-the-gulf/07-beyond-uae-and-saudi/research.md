# 07 · AI beyond the UAE and Saudi Arabia: research

Researched 2026-11-26. Source ids refer to `items.json` (prefix `07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-`). Every fact below comes from a page that was fetched; figures are given as the page gives them.

## Facts

### Qatar: capital plus an Arabic model
1. Qatar's national AI strategy began as a blueprint by the Qatar Computing Research Institute (QCRI) at Hamad Bin Khalifa University. The Minister of Transport and Communications announced it as the national strategy at QITCOM in October 2019. The ministry called it the first strategy of its kind written jointly by a government and a research institute [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-06].
2. The 2019 strategy has six pillars: Talent; Data Access; Employment; Wealth Creation; Embracing an AI + X Future; and Thought Leadership in AI Ethics [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-06].
3. PwC says Qatar aims to become a leading regional AI hub by 2030, guided by Qatar National Vision 2030, the Third National Development Strategy 2024-2030 and MCIT's Digital Agenda 2030 [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-09].
4. In May 2024 Qatar's communications minister referred to the Amir's 9 billion QR incentive package for digital transformation, plus a billion-dollar fund for regional entrepreneurs and emerging companies [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-05].
5. In December 2025 Brookfield and Qai, the AI company of the Qatar Investment Authority (QIA), announced a $20 billion joint venture for AI infrastructure in Qatar and in some international markets [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-01].
6. The venture plans fully integrated AI facilities and an "Integrated Compute center" that gives the region access to high-performance compute. Brookfield's CEO called it Brookfield's first AI infrastructure investment in the Middle East [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-01].
7. Brookfield invests through its AI Infrastructure Fund. The Qai deal is a cornerstone of a Brookfield programme that aims to raise up to $100 billion of investment worldwide [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-01].
8. QIA invested in Anthropic's $13 billion Series F in September 2025, at a $183 billion post-money valuation [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-07].
9. QIA says it joined Anthropic's $65 billion Series H in May 2026, its third straight round in the company, and that the round valued Anthropic at $965 billion post-money [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-08].
10. PwC lists more pieces of Qatar's build-out: plans to deploy advanced GPU capacity with MEEZA, Microsoft's 2024 expansion of its Qatar cloud region, and GPU-backed Azure OpenAI services set up with MCIT [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-09].
11. PwC reports that MCIT figures from October 2026 showed more than 8,000 active users in its Microsoft 365 Copilot programme and 96 AI agent use cases [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-09].
12. Fanar is Qatar's Arabic AI platform. QCRI built it entirely in-house with sponsorship from the Ministry of Communications and IT, to develop sovereign AI technology [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-03].
13. The first Fanar paper (January 2025) describes two models. Fanar Star has 7B parameters and was trained from scratch on nearly 1 trillion Arabic, English and code tokens. Fanar Prime has 9B parameters and was built on Google's Gemma-2 9B [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-03].
14. The Fanar platform adds an Islamic retrieval system for religious questions, speech recognition for several Arabic dialects, and an attribution service for checking facts [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-03].
15. The open model card for Fanar-1-9B lists an Apache 2.0 licence and support for Modern Standard Arabic plus Gulf, Levantine and Egyptian dialects [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-04].
16. QCRI announced Fanar 2.0 at World Summit AI in Doha on 9 December 2025. It has 27 billion parameters, against 9 billion for the original from December 2024 [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-02].
17. QCRI's executive director said work on Fanar 3.0 had begun, with release planned for December 2026 [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-02].

### Kuwait: the Microsoft partnership route
18. In March 2025 Microsoft signed a strategic partnership with Kuwait's government, represented by the Central Agency for IT (CAIT) and the regulator CITRA. Microsoft said it intends to build an "AI-powered Azure Region" in Kuwait [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-10].
19. Under the same deal, Kuwaiti government employees are to get Microsoft 365 Copilot, and Microsoft is to set up an AI Innovation Center and a Cloud Center of Excellence [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-10].
20. In November 2026 Microsoft opened its Digital Innovation Center in Kuwait, tied to New Kuwait Vision 2035 [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-12].
21. In June 2025 DatacenterDynamics, citing Bloomberg, reported that the Kuwait Investment Authority would become the first non-founding financial anchor of the AI Infrastructure Partnership founded by BlackRock with Microsoft and MGX. That partnership aims to raise up to $30bn in equity plus about $70bn in debt. KIA's contribution was not disclosed [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-11].
22. According to the AI Regulation Map tracker (November 2026), Kuwait's National AI Strategy 2025-2028 is still a draft and Kuwait has no AI-specific law that imposes obligations [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-13].
23. The same tracker says Ministerial Decision No. 159 of 2026 made "AI services and solutions" a licensable business activity. A bill to create a Public Authority for AI lapsed when the National Assembly was dissolved in May 2024 [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-13].

### Bahrain: rules first
24. Bahrain's Information & eGovernment Authority launched a National Policy for the Use of AI and adopted the GCC Guiding Manual on the Ethics of AI Use (July 2025) [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-14].
25. The Bahraini policy has four pillars: legal compliance, AI use and adoption, public education and awareness, and local and international cooperation. It is tied to the Personal Data Protection Law and Bahrain Economic Vision 2030 [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-14].

### Oman: a programme, a dialect model and a zone
26. Oman launched its National Programme for AI and Advanced Digital Technologies in September 2024. It covers 2024 to 2026 and upgrades an executive programme from 2022 [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-15].
27. The programme plans an open-data platform, an AI research and development centre, an AI "studio", and an Omani language model grounded in Omani cultural content [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-15].
28. Oman's stated targets are a place among the top 50 countries in the Government AI Readiness Index, and a digital-economy share of GDP of 10 percent by 2040, up from 2 percent in 2021 [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-15].
29. In September 2025 Oman announced the Omani language model "Maeen", the Omani AI Studio and a National Open Data Portal [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-16].
30. Royal Decree No. 50/2026 (April/May 2026) set up an AI Special Zone of about 104,000 square metres in Seeb, Muscat, aimed at semiconductors, robotics, AI and startups [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-17].

### Egypt: talent scale and a national model
31. Egypt's National Council for AI launched the second edition of the National AI Strategy (2025-2030). It has six pillars (governance, technology, data, infrastructure, ecosystem, talent) and 21 initiatives [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-18].
32. The initiatives include national AI models for local challenges, high-quality Arabic datasets, a national data centre for AI, and a national AI certification system [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-18].
33. Egypt's 2030 targets are an ICT share of GDP of 7.7%, more than 250 AI startups and more than 30,000 trained AI specialists [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-19].
34. When the strategy launched, Microsoft pledged to train and certify 100,000 people in AI in Egypt, and Capgemini announced an AI Center of Excellence in Cairo [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-19].
35. Fast Company Middle East reports that Egypt aims for an estimated $42.7bn in AI-related economic value by 2030 [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-21].
36. Egypt launched Karnak, its first nationally developed large language model named after Karnak Temple, at AI Everything MEA in Cairo in February 2026. Officials said it is the top-ranked Arabic model in the 30-40 billion parameter class [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-21].
37. On 23 August 2026 Egypt's Ministry of Communications and IT (MCIT) launched a pilot of "Karnak Chat". Karnak was built by enhancing open-source models. It targets Modern Standard Arabic and Egyptian colloquial Arabic, can run inside local data centres, and a free version is on Hugging Face [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-20].

### Jordan: delivery and Arabic content
38. Jordan's AI Strategy and Implementation Plan (2023-2027) has 68 projects. Minister Sami Smeirat said in October 2026 that 42 of them (about 62%) had reached implementation or completion [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-22].
39. Jordan's 2025 progress report counts 14 projects completed and 28 underway. The priority sectors are education, energy, water, agriculture, government services and transport [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-22].
40. Jordanian examples include the Siraj education platform, forecasting of electrical load, water efficiency in farming, and AI traffic management [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-22].
41. Mawdoo3 is a Jordanian company focused on Arabic AI and large language models that owns more than 200 Arabic websites. It raised $25 million across three rounds, and in November 2024 Saudi-based Naif Al-Rajhi Investment took an undisclosed strategic stake [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-23].

### Türkiye: the biggest numbers, its own models
42. Türkiye published its AI Action Plan 2026-2030 on 18 August 2026 through Presidential Circular No. 2026/9. The plan has 16 actions under four pillars: Recognise, Benefit, Produce and Govern [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-24].
43. The plan's targets are 10,000 advanced AI specialists, 100,000 AI application professionals, at least 1 GW of AI and data-centre capacity, and at least USD 10 billion of mainly private investment [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-24][07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-25].
44. It also sets up a National AI Research Fund of at least TRY 10 billion, an AI Growth Fund of TRY 15 billion, at least five AI Growth Zones by end-2028, and a National AI Board chaired by the President [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-24].
45. Minister Kacır said the plan includes AI literacy training for 5 million people, and that the first version of BİLGE, a domestically built Turkish-language model, would soon open to developers [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-25].
46. T3AI was built by the T3 Foundation and the defence firm Baykar with contributions from 1,792 volunteers, and was released as an open-source beta in July 2025 [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-26].
47. Kumru, from the startup VNGRS, is a 7.4-billion-parameter Turkish model that runs on a 16 GB consumer GPU. It is aimed at regulated sectors that need AI running on their own premises, and it went viral for wrong answers in its early test version [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-27].
48. Defence firm HAVELSAN's MAIN platform contains a 9-billion-parameter model focused on Turkish. Turkcell and Google Cloud data centres in Ankara are expected to be fully operational by 2028 [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-28].

### Morocco: sovereignty, dialects and renewable-powered compute
49. "Morocco AI 2030" targets by 2030 are 100 billion dirhams ($10 billion) of added value to GDP, 50,000 jobs, and 200,000 trained and certified AI talents [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-29].
50. Morocco's plan runs through four JAZARI institutes: ROOT, Smart City, EduTech and Industrie X.0. It also includes a sovereign AI marketplace and the "Idarati AI" assistant for administrative procedures [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-29].
51. Morocco plans close to 1GW of data-centre capacity, including a Dakhla complex of up to 500MW on renewable energy, and a large local model that understands Darija and Amazigh. Named partners include Mistral AI, Oracle, Nokia and Orange Business [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-30].

## Conflicts and dates

- **Fanar size and training data.** The arXiv paper (Jan 2025) gives Fanar Prime as 9B and Fanar Star as 7B, trained on "nearly 1 trillion" tokens. The Hugging Face card lists Fanar-1-9B at 8.7 billion parameters with 1T tokens (515B English, 410B Arabic, 102B code). Middle East AI News (Dec 2025) says the original was trained on 1.3 trillion tokens (40% Arabic, 50% English, 10% code). On air, use "about 9 billion parameters" and "about a trillion tokens", attributed to QCRI's paper. Fanar 3.0 was due "December 2026", so check before publishing whether it has shipped.
- **Qatar incentive package.** MCIT (May 2024) says "9 billion QR". Search snippets from the US trade.gov guide and the IMF gave $2.4bn and $2.5bn, but those pages were not fetched. Say "QR 9 billion" only.
- **Egypt targets.** TechAfrica says "250+ AI-driven startups". Other summaries (not fetched) say "AI companies". The $42.7bn value figure comes from Fast Company ME, not the strategy document itself, so attribute it.
- **Karnak size.** Officials gave a ranking "in the 30-40 billion parameter category", and a secondary site said "30-80 billion". The exact parameter count was not confirmed.
- **Anthropic valuations move fast.** $183bn (Sep 2025) and $965bn (May 2026) are QIA's own figures for each round. Date-stamp both on screen.
- **Türkiye has two plans.** The 2024-2025 Action Plan (71 actions, not sourced in items) has been superseded by the 2026-2030 plan (16 actions). Use only the 2026-2030 plan. The $10bn and 1GW figures appear in both CMS and Hürriyet, so they are consistent.
- **Oman's AI programme** runs 2024 to 2026 and so ends about now. A successor may be announced soon.
- **Kuwait's strategy** was still a draft as of 2 Nov 2026, per the tracker. Recheck its status.
- **Evergreen risk.** Fanar 3.0, BİLGE availability, Karnak's move from pilot to full release, and Morocco's Dakhla build are all likely to change within a year. Phrase each as "announced" or "planned".

## Proposed outline

**Working title:** AI Beyond UAE & Saudi: Qatar, Egypt, Türkiye and More (55 chars)

**First 15 seconds:** "Everyone talks about Abu Dhabi and Riyadh. But Qatar is putting $20 billion into AI infrastructure, Egypt has launched its own Arabic model, and Türkiye wants a gigawatt of compute. Here is what each country is really betting on."

**Chapter 1: Qatar, the investor with an Arabic voice (about 3 min).** The 2019 QCRI-written strategy, the QR 9bn incentive, the $20bn Brookfield-Qai venture, QIA's Anthropic rounds, and Fanar's path from 9B to 27B to a planned 3.0. Facts 1-17.

**Chapter 2: The smaller Gulf states, three different playbooks (about 2.5 min).** Kuwait partners with Microsoft (Azure region, Copilot, KIA in the AIP) while its strategy is still a draft. Jordan's 68 projects, 62% of them in progress or done, the practical sector projects, and Mawdoo3 as the Arabic-content pioneer. Facts 31-41.

**Chapter 4: Türkiye, the industrial-scale plan (about 2.5 min).** The 2026-2030 plan (16 actions, 1GW, $10bn, 100,000 professionals, the AI Board under the President), and its home-grown models (T3AI, Kumru, BİLGE, HAVELSAN), with the defence industry as an engine. Facts 42-48.

**Chapter 5: Morocco and the scorecard (about 2 min).** Morocco AI 2030 (MAD 100bn, the JAZARI institutes, Darija and Amazigh models, renewable-powered Dakhla). **Analogy for the hardest idea, buy compute versus build a model:** Think of a country deciding on a restaurant. Renting a famous chef's kitchen (a Microsoft Azure region, a Brookfield data centre) gets you serving fast. Writing your own recipe book in your own dialect (Fanar, Karnak, Maeen) takes longer but means the menu tastes like home and the recipes stay in the family. Most of these countries are doing a bit of both.

## Shorts

1. **"Qatar's $20 billion AI bet."** Fact: QIA's AI company Qai and Brookfield formed a $20bn venture for AI infrastructure in Qatar and abroad [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-01]. Takeaway: Qatar is using its sovereign wealth to buy a seat at the compute table, not just to buy shares in AI companies.
2. **"Egypt built its own ChatGPT-style assistant."** Fact: Egypt's MCIT piloted Karnak Chat in August 2026. It is built on the national Karnak model, tuned for Modern Standard Arabic and Egyptian colloquial Arabic, and can run in local data centres [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-20]. Takeaway: for governments, "sovereign AI" often means "it understands how we speak, and our data stays home."
3. **"Türkiye's gigawatt goal."** Fact: Türkiye's 2026-2030 AI plan targets at least 1 GW of data-centre capacity and at least $10bn of mainly private investment by 2030 [07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-24][07-beyond-uae-and-saudi-25]. Takeaway: outside the Gulf, the region's most ambitious compute target comes from Ankara.
