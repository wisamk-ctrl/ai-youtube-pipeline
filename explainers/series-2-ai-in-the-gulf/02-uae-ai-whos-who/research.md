# 02: Who's who of AI in the UAE (research)

Researched 2026-11-26. Source ids refer to `items.json` (prefix `02-uae-ai-whos-who-`, shortened below to the number, e.g. [11] = `02-uae-ai-whos-who-11`). Every fact below is from a page fetched for this episode.

## Facts

### The federal layer
1. Omar Sultan Al Olama was appointed the UAE's Minister of State for Artificial Intelligence in October 2017; in July 2020 the title became Minister of State for Artificial Intelligence, Digital Economy and Remote Work Applications [28].
2. A US Commerce Department release says the UAE was the first country to appoint a federal Minister of AI, in 2017 [07].
3. The UAE announced its National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence 2031 in October 2017; its aim is for the UAE to be one of the world's AI leaders by 2031 [29].
4. The strategy has eight objectives, among them adopting AI across government services, attracting and training talent, and ensuring strong governance and effective regulation. The UAE AI Office is responsible for developing and carrying it out. OECD.AI records it as "not evaluated" [29].
5. Al Olama's office is responsible for the goals of both the National AI Strategy and the UAE Digital Economy Strategy. He is also Deputy Managing Director of the Dubai Future Foundation, which is one link between the federal and Dubai layers [28].

### Abu Dhabi: the council at the top
6. On 22 January 2024 Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed, acting as Ruler of Abu Dhabi, issued a law setting up the Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Technology Council (AIATC). It sets policy and strategy for AI research, infrastructure and investment in Abu Dhabi [11].
7. The AIATC's founding line-up: Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed (Chairman), Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed (Vice Chairman), and as members Khaldoon Khalifa Al Mubarak, Jassem Mohamed Bu Ataba Al Zaabi, Faisal Abdulaziz Al Bannai and G42 CEO Peng Xiao [11].
8. The same people sit across the system. Tahnoon chairs the AIATC, G42 and MGX. Khaldoon Al Mubarak (Mubadala's MD and Group CEO) is on the AIATC, is MGX vice-chair, sits on the G42 board and chairs MBZUAI's board of trustees. Peng Xiao is on the AIATC, the MGX board and the MBZUAI board [11][12][25][30][22].

### G42, the operator
9. Fortune reports that G42 was founded in 2018 and is named after *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*. It describes Sheikh Tahnoon, the UAE national security adviser, as chair and controlling shareholder [24].
10. G42 lists its investors as Mubadala, Silver Lake, Microsoft and the Dalio Family Office [22]. In November 2025 an Abu Dhabi Media Office readout called Microsoft G42's "minority shareholder" and listed the board as Tahnoon (chair), Khaldoon Al Mubarak, Jassem Al Zaabi, Brad Smith, Egon Durban, Peng Xiao and Martin Edelman [25].
11. On 16 October 2023 G42 launched Core42, formed by merging three units: G42 Cloud, Inception and Injazat. Group CTO Kiril Evtimov became Core42's CEO [16].
12. Middle East AI News adds the origins of those three units: G42 Cloud was set up in 2021, Inception in 2018 (formerly the Inception Institute of Artificial Intelligence), and Injazat was acquired in 2020 through an equity deal with Mubadala [17].
13. In August 2024 G42 spun Inception back out as a separate entity for AI model development, headed by Andrew Jackson. It released 20 open-source Jais models in 8 sizes, from 590M to 70B parameters, under Apache 2.0 [18].
14. In 2026 Core42 raised USD 550 million in non-dilutive HSBC trade finance (USD 240 million in February, USD 310 million in May) for AI compute in the US and Europe. Its European operations are run from a Dublin headquarters [19].
15. Presight, G42's big-data analytics company, listed on the Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange in March 2023. The IPO was 136x oversubscribed and raised USD 496 million [20].
16. In September 2025 G42 sold a 2 per cent stake in Presight for $100 million and kept about 68.5 per cent, down from 70.5 per cent [21].
17. Presight says it bought 51% of AIQ, ADNOC's industrial AI venture, in an AED 4.9B deal. It reports FY-25 revenue of AED 3.03B (+36.9% YoY) [20].
18. Khazna Data Centers lists its shareholders as G42, MGX and Silver Lake [23]. G42 calls Khazna "a subsidiary of G42" [03].
19. Mubadala says G42 and Mubadala together developed Khazna, launched M42 (healthcare technology) and created Space42 (geospatial) [12]. Space42 was formed by merging Yahsat and Bayanat [13].
20. G42 describes itself as a "neocloud enterprise born in Abu Dhabi" building what it calls the "Intelligence Grid" [03].

### The money: MGX, Mubadala, ADQ
21. The AIATC announced MGX on 11 March 2024 as a technology investment company with Mubadala and G42 as founding partners. It focuses on three areas: AI infrastructure, semiconductors, and AI core technologies and applications [12].
22. Sheikh Tahnoun chairs MGX and Khaldoon Al Mubarak is vice-chair. Ahmed Yahia Al Idrissi, previously head of Mubadala's Direct Investments platform, is CEO [12].
23. Mubadala reports AED 1.2 trillion in assets under management for 2024 (a US$330 billion portfolio). It calls MGX Abu Dhabi's "new AI investment champion" [13].
24. Mubadala says MGX co-led the US$30 billion AI Infrastructure Partnership (AIP) with Microsoft, BlackRock, GIP, NVIDIA and xAI, and invested in OpenAI, Anthropic and Databricks [13]. MGX says AIP was established in September 2024 [15].
25. On July 21, 2026 AIP, MGX and BlackRock's GIP closed the purchase of Aligned Data Centers at an enterprise value of about $40 billion: 51 campuses and more than 6.4GW of operational and planned capacity. It is AIP's first investment [14].
26. ADQ, Abu Dhabi's other big state investor, is in AI mainly through power. In March 2025 it agreed a 50-50 partnership with Energy Capital Partners to invest more than US$25 billion across 25 GW of power projects for data centres, mostly in the US [31].

### The research side: ATRC, TII, AI71, MBZUAI
27. The Advanced Technology Research Council (ATRC) was set up in 2020 and is chaired by Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed. By February 2024 it had 10 research centres and 851 researchers from more than 70 countries [27].
28. The Technology Innovation Institute (TII) is ATRC's applied research arm. It built Falcon, which the Abu Dhabi Media Office calls the UAE's first generative AI LLM, and open-sourced Falcon 40B and 180B [27].
29. On 28 November 2023 ATRC launched AI71 to commercialise TII's Falcon models (7B, 40B and 180B) through its VentureOne subsidiary. It started with the medical, educational and legal sectors and offers clients self-hosting so they keep control of their data [26].
30. MBZUAI was established in 2019 in Masdar City, Abu Dhabi. It calls itself the world's first graduate-level, research-based AI university [30].
31. Eric Xing was named MBZUAI's founding president in November 2020. The first cohort, 78 graduate students from 29 countries, arrived in January 2021, and the first undergraduates arrived in August 2025 [30].
32. G42, Microsoft and MBZUAI founded the Responsible AI Future Foundation in Abu Dhabi "in February", according to Microsoft's November 2025 post [02].

### Dubai's lane
33. On 8-9 June 2023 Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed, Crown Prince of Dubai, launched the Dubai Centre for Artificial Intelligence under the Dubai Future Foundation. It aimed to train 1,000 government employees from over 30 entities in generative AI [32].
34. Dubai's Universal Blueprint for AI (reported 3 May 2024 as recently unveiled) is a yearly plan tied to the D33 agenda. The target is AED100 billion a year from the digital economy and a 50 percent rise in productivity [33][34].
35. Phase one of the Blueprint includes a Chief AI Officer in every Dubai government entity (candidates are assessed by the Dubai Centre for AI), AI and Web3 incubators, a Dubai commercial licence for AI, and fast-tracked land for data centres [33].
36. The division of labour, as these sources describe it: Dubai concentrates on adopting AI in government services and attracting companies [32][33], while Abu Dhabi's institutions own the compute, capital and research (AIATC, MGX, G42, ATRC) [11][12][27]. (This is a framing drawn from the sources, not a quote from one.)

### The American partnerships
37. On April 16, 2024 Microsoft announced a $1.5 billion strategic investment in G42. Brad Smith joined G42's board, and G42 agreed to run its AI applications on Azure [01].
38. The deal sits under an Intergovernmental Assurance Agreement developed with the US and UAE governments. Microsoft says it covers cybersecurity, export controls, KYC and responsible AI [01][02].
39. Bloomberg reports that G42 had promised to divest from Huawei, which cleared the way for the Microsoft deal [09]. Earlier, Fortune relayed a New York Times report that US officials had pushed G42 to cut ties with Chinese firms; G42 said it was replacing a "legacy technology supplier, which included Chinese hardware" [24].
40. Microsoft says it will invest $15.2 billion in the UAE from 2023 to 2029: just over $7.3 billion through end-2025, including the G42 stake, and more than $7.9 billion in 2026-2029 [02].
41. Microsoft says its Biden-era export licences brought the equivalent of 21,500 Nvidia A100 GPUs into the UAE. Licences approved in September 2025 add the equivalent of 60,400 A100s, using GB300 chips [02].
42. In November 2025 Microsoft and G42 announced a 200MW datacenter expansion through Khazna. It is expected to start coming online before end-2026 [03].
43. On 19 November 2025 the US Commerce Department approved chip sales to G42 itself: compute equivalent to 35,000 Nvidia GB300s, conditional on "rigorous security and reporting requirements". Earlier approvals had covered US firms but not G42 [09].
44. On May 15, 2025 the 5GW UAE-US AI Campus was unveiled in Abu Dhabi under the "US-UAE AI Acceleration Partnership". It covers 10 square miles, G42 builds it, and its compute is reserved for US hyperscalers and approved cloud providers [07].
45. The White House says that under the May 2025 AI agreement the UAE committed to invest in, build or finance US data centers "at least as large and as powerful" as those in the UAE [08].
46. Stargate UAE is a 1GW cluster on that campus. G42 (through Khazna) builds it, OpenAI and Oracle operate it, NVIDIA supplies GB300 systems, and Cisco and SoftBank are partners. The first 200MW is expected to go live in 2026 [04][05][06].
47. OpenAI calls Stargate UAE its first "OpenAI for Countries" partnership and says the UAE will be the first country to enable ChatGPT nationwide [04].
48. In September 2026 Reuters reported, citing six people familiar, that the UAE is revising the 5GW campus after the Iran war. The campus would likely become a network of sites across the UAE, with air defences and some facilities underground. G42 said work is "progressing as planned" and that specifics are "subject to continuous review" [10].
49. Reuters puts Stargate UAE's first phase at $30 billion. Construction began in 2025, with 200MW due online in 2026 [10].

## Conflicts and dates

- **The campus is being redesigned.** The May 2025 releases describe one 10-square-mile campus [05][07]. In September 2026 Reuters reported it would likely become a distributed network [10]. The Reuters report is newer, but it rests on anonymous sources and G42 did not confirm a change. On air, attribute it to Reuters and present it as a plan under review.
- **Stargate's 200MW go-live.** Every source says "2026" [04][05][06][10]. None of the fetched pages confirms that it is live as of 2026-11-26. Check before recording, and keep it out of the evergreen script.
- **$30 billion for Stargate UAE** comes only from Reuters [10]. The G42 and OpenAI releases give no cost.
- **How far the campus reaches.** OpenAI says compute within a 2,000-mile radius reaching "up to half the world's population" [04]. The US release says "nearly half" the global population lives within 3,200 km (2,000 miles) [07]. The two agree; use the US wording.
- **Inception's status changed twice.** It was merged into Core42 in October 2023 [16][17] and relaunched as a separate entity in August 2024 [18]. Don't call it "part of Core42" today.
- **G42's Presight stake** fell from 70.5% to about 68.5% in September 2025 [21]. It may have changed since.
- **Microsoft's GPU figures** come as two A100-equivalent numbers (21,500 and 60,400) [02]. Quote them as given rather than as a total.
- **Microsoft's stake in G42** is described only as "minority" [25]. No percentage was found.
- **AIP size.** Mubadala calls it "the US$30 billion AI Infrastructure Partnership" [13]. Aligned and MGX describe $30 billion as an equity target, with up to $100 billion including debt [14][15]. Say "aims to mobilise $30 billion of equity".
- **Founding chair of the MBZUAI board.** Sultan Al Jaber chaired the founding board (2019); Khaldoon Al Mubarak has chaired the new board since February 2025 [30].
- **G42's founding year (2018)** comes from Fortune [24], not from a G42 page.
- **Dubai Blueprint launch date.** The Dubai government page gives no date; IndiaAI reported it on 3 May 2024 as "recently unveiled" [34]. Say "spring 2024", or check the exact date (Middle East AI News snippet, not fetched, said April 2024).
- **Likely to change:** board seats and titles (Al Olama's portfolio has already changed once), MGX's portfolio, Core42 financing, Presight figures, chip-licence volumes, and the campus layout.

## Proposed outline

**Working title:** Who Runs AI in the UAE? G42, MGX & MBZUAI Explained (52 characters)

**First 15 seconds:** "One man chairs the UAE's AI council, its biggest AI company and its AI investment fund. In 12 minutes you'll have a map of how the whole UAE AI system fits together: who sets the rules, who holds the money, who builds, and who the Americans are betting on."

**Chapter 1: Two layers of government (about 2 min).** The 2017 federal minister and AI Strategy 2031, the eight objectives, and the AI Office; then Abu Dhabi's AIATC (January 2024) and who sits on it. Facts 1-8.

**Chapter 2: G42, a family of companies (about 3 min).** Founding in 2018, ownership and board; Core42 (the merger, and Inception spun back out), Presight (IPO and stake sale), Khazna (data centres and shareholders), M42 and Space42. Facts 9-20.

**Chapter 3: The money and the brains (about 2.5 min).** MGX (founders, three focus areas, AIP, Aligned at $40bn), Mubadala's scale, ADQ's power play; then ATRC, TII and Falcon, AI71, MBZUAI and RAIFF. Facts 21-32.

**Chapter 4: Dubai's lane (about 1.5 min).** The Dubai Centre for AI, the Universal Blueprint, Chief AI Officers and the AI licence: adoption and attraction rather than chips. Facts 33-36.

**Chapter 5: The American bargain (about 3 min).** Microsoft's $1.5bn and the IGAA, the Huawei exit, the $15.2bn plan and GPU licences, Commerce approving G42 directly, the 5GW campus and Stargate UAE, the reciprocal US data-centre pledge, and the 2026 redesign reported by Reuters. Facts 37-49.

**Analogy for the hardest idea (how council, fund and operator interlock):** Think of a family-run airline group. The board (AIATC) decides strategy. The treasury (Mubadala and MGX) buys stakes in other airlines and in plane makers abroad. The operating airline (G42 and its units Core42, Khazna and Presight) actually flies the routes. The flight school (MBZUAI) and the engineering workshop (TII) train pilots and design parts. The same few family members sit on every board, so decisions move fast.

- A timeline card from 2017 to 2026: minister and strategy (2017), G42 (2018), MBZUAI (2019), ATRC (2020), Presight IPO and DCAI (2023), Core42 and AI71 (2023), AIATC and MGX (2024), Microsoft's $1.5bn (2024), Stargate UAE (2025), Aligned closes (2026).
- A UAE map with Abu Dhabi (compute, capital, research) and Dubai (adoption, licensing) side by side.
- Concentric rings: a 5GW campus containing 1GW Stargate UAE containing the first 200MW.
- A stacked bar of Microsoft's $15.2bn: $7.3bn+ (2023-2025) and $7.9bn+ (2026-2029).

**What to watch next:** Will the UAE's AI campus stay one giant site near Abu Dhabi, or turn into a hardened network spread across the Emirates? And does that change who wants to put their compute there?
