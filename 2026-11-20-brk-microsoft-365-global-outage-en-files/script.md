[Section: Microsoft 365 Outage Goes Global]

[Visual: stock office tower at night, floors going dark]
A Microsoft 365 outage took down Outlook, Teams and other services around the world.

[Visual: number 7.7 million | outage reports in 60 hours]
Downdetector logged more than 7.7 million reports in the 60 hours after it began, Broadband Breakfast reports.

[Visual: stock data center corridor, blue light]
More than 90 countries and territories logged at least 1,000 reports. Japan's Digital Agency could not receive email.

[Visual: title UK Hospitals Back to Paper]
In the UK, almost all NHS Trusts lost email, and some operations were cancelled, Tech Policy Press reports.

[Visual: stock network traffic jamming at a gateway ring]
Microsoft's preliminary review blames a software defect, introduced in July, in its network gateway servers.

[Visual: stock server hall with one dark row of racks]
At worst, more than 80% of those servers stopped responding. Microsoft found no sign of a cyberattack.

[Visual: stock three data centers linked by bridges of light]
For the Gulf, watch how much businesses and governments rely on a single cloud provider.
